## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet anticipation, mindful celebration, and botanical calm. Rooted in the visual philosophy of Material 3 Expressive, it reimagines personal milestones—not as frantic deadlines, but as restorative, meaningful horizons.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Quiet Grounding:** Calm, collected tones cultivate peace rather than urgency.
- **Organic Clarity:** Soft vegetal hues evoke natural rhythms, seasonal growth, and breathing room.
- **Expressive Tactility:** Responsive morphing geometries and warm golden accents surface celebration without noise.

### Style Direction
- **Material 3 Expressive:** Dynamic color harmony, pill-shaped chips, floating contextual containers, expressive asymmetrical hero shapes, and rich botanical surfaces.
- **Atmosphere:** Natural light diffusion with tonal-layer separation, eliminating harsh dark shadows in favor of ambient sage-tinted depth.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in deep evergreen teals, balanced by soft botanical mints and warm golden nectar highlights reserved for celebratory milestones and badges.

### Color Tokens & Roles

- **Primary (`#006B5B`):** Deep serene forest teal. Applied to primary hero callouts, principal action buttons, filled FABs, and prominent countdown digits.
  - `on-primary`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `primary-container`: `#A6F2D9` (Soft mint sage)
  - `on-primary-container`: `#00201A`
- **Secondary (`#4C6359`):** Grounded eucalyptus sage. Used for secondary navigation, filtering chips, and muted countdown subtitles.
  - `on-secondary`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `secondary-container`: `#D4E8DF` (Tranquil soft sage)
  - `on-secondary-container`: `#081F17`
- **Tertiary (`#845400`):** Warm burnished honey. Used intentionally for anniversary highlights, streak flames, and milestone badges.
  - `on-tertiary`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `tertiary-container`: `#FFDDB2` (Warm golden peach / honey)
  - `on-tertiary-container`: `#2A1800`
- **Surfaces & Backgrounds:**
  - `surface`: `#F6FAF7` (Base background canvas)
  - `surface-dim`: `#D5DDD7`
  - `surface-bright`: `#F6FAF7`
  - `surface-container-lowest`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `surface-container-low`: `#F0F5F2`
  - `surface-container`: `#EDF5F0` (Default card surface)
  - `surface-container-high`: `#E5EEE8` (Sheet & elevated surface)
  - `surface-container-highest`: `#DFE8E2` (Input fields & search tracks)
  - `on-surface`: `#171D1A` (Primary body text & icons)
  - `on-surface-variant`: `#404944` (Secondary captions & inactive icons)
  - `outline`: `#707973` (Standard component borders)
  - `outline-variant`: `#BFC9C2` (Subtle dividers & structural boundaries)

## Typography

The type system relies completely on **Roboto Flex** to deliver fluid variable capabilities across numerical display figures and quiet editorial copy.

- **Numerical Display:** Large countdown indicators use `display-lg` or `display-lg-mobile` with tabular figures (`tnum`) to eliminate jitter during real-time tick changes.
- **Optical Consistency:** Headlines leverage tighter tracking with medium-bold weights (`wght 600`), establishing an organic connection with Material 3 Expressive's bold rounded components.
- **Hierarchy Mapping:**
  - Remaining days/hours counter: `display-lg-mobile` (44px) / `display-lg` (57px).
  - Milestone Card Titles: `title-lg` (22px) or `headline-sm` (24px).
  - Metric sub-labels ("DAYS REMAINING", "HOURS"): `label-sm` (11px uppercase) with letter-spacing of `0.5px`.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid vertical layout system built around an 8dp grid increment, tuned for natural single-hand thumb reach on modern handhelds.

### Layout Model
- **Mobile Grid (Primary):** 4-column layout with `16px` outer screen margins and `16px` gutter.
- **Tablet / Large Foldable Grid:** 8-column layout with `24px` outer margins, allowing hero countdown cards to occupy a 4-column span side-by-side with timeline lists.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 
  - Standard intra-card element spacing uses `spacing-xs` (8px) and `spacing-md` (16px).
  - Major section gaps and staggered list flows use `spacing-xl` (32px).
- **Safe Area Considerations:** Bottom navigation bars anchor with an expanded floating bar clearance of `80px` plus bottom system inset, preventing overlay collisions with Floating Action Buttons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed primarily through **Tonal Surface Stacking** complemented by diffused, mint-tinted ambient shadows.

### Tonal Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base window color is `surface` (`#F6FAF7`).
2. **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Lists):** `surface-container` (`#EDF5F0`), zero shadow, distinguished by clean tonal boundary.
3. **Level 2 (Active/Selected Cards & Sheets):** `surface-container-high` (`#E5EEE8`), elevated with an ambient shadow: `0px 4px 12px rgba(0, 50, 40, 0.04)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Dialogs, Contextual Menus):** `surface-container-highest` (`#DFE8E2`) with an ambient drop: `0px 8px 24px rgba(0, 32, 26, 0.08)`.
5. **Floating Action Elements (Primary FAB):** Backed by `primary` or `primaryContainer`, utilizing dynamic elevation:
   - Resting: `0px 4px 8px rgba(0, 107, 91, 0.15)`
   - Pressed: `0px 2px 4px rgba(0, 107, 91, 0.2)`

### Outlines
Borders are strictly low-contrast (`outline-variant` `#BFC9C2` at 1px thickness) used only when cards sit on contrasting image media or in zero-elevation edge scenarios.

## Shapes

In accordance with Material 3 Expressive and `ROUND_EIGHT` parameters, shapes utilize soft-to-prominent curvature with foundational radii based on multiples of 8dp.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Geometry:**
  - Extra Small (Buttons, small badges): `8px` (`0.5rem`)
  - Small (Inputs, filter chips, snackbars): `12px` (`0.75rem`)
  - Medium (Standard countdown cards, list items): `16px` (`1rem`)
  - Large (Hero countdown card, bottom sheets): `28px` (`1.75rem`)
  - Full / Pill (Pill chips, Primary Floating Action Buttons): `9999px`

## Components

### Buttons
- **Filled Primary:** Background `primary` (`#006B5B`), text/icon `on-primary` (`#FFFFFF`), height `48px`, shape radius `9999px` (pill).
- **Tonal Button:** Background `secondary-container` (`#D4E8DF`), text `on-secondary-container` (`#081F17`), shape radius `9999px`.
- **Celebration Action:** Background `tertiary-container` (`#FFDDB2`), text `on-tertiary-container` (`#2A1800`), used when configuring anniversary events or marking completion.

### Countdown Cards (Core Experience)
- **Hero Milestone Card:** 
  - Background: `primary-container` (`#A6F2D9`).
  - Shape: Rounded `28px`.
  - Content: Display numeric counter rendered in `on-primary-container` (`#00201A`) with `display-lg-mobile`. Unit labels set to uppercase `label-sm`.
  - Trailing corner accommodates an optional contextual celebration chip.
- **Standard Countdown Card:**
  - Background: `surface-container` (`#EDF5F0`).
  - Shape: Rounded `16px`.
  - Padding: `16px`.
  - Border: None, relying on tonal contrast against `surface`.

### Chips & Tags
- **Filter / Event Type Chips:** Height `32px`, corner radius `8px` to `9999px`. 
  - Unselected: Background `surface-container-high` (`#E5EEE8`), label `on-surface-variant` (`#404944`).
  - Selected: Background `secondary-container` (`#D4E8DF`), icon and label `on-secondary-container` (`#081F17`).
- **Anniversary & Milestone Badges:** Height `24px`, background `tertiary-container` (`#FFDDB2`), text `on-tertiary-container` (`#2A1800`), font `label-sm`.

### Input Fields
- **Text & Date Fields:** Filled style with background `surface-container-highest` (`#DFE8E2`).
- Shape: Rounded top corners `12px`, with an active bottom stroke `2px` in `primary` (`#006B5B`).
- Label: Floating label transitioning from `body-lg` to `label-sm` in `primary`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **States:** Checked states filled in `primary` (`#006B5B`) with an `on-primary` (`#FFFFFF`) checkmark. Unchecked state consists of a `2px` border in `outline` (`#707973`).
- Touch Target: Inset visual box with a minimum `48px × 48px` accessible ripple target area.

### Progress & Time Arcs
- **Countdown Circular Indicators:** Track background `surface-container-highest` (`#DFE8E2`), active arc indicator `primary` (`#006B5B`) with rounded stroke caps. Milestone completion transitions the indicator into `tertiary` (`#845400`).
